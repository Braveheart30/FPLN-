FPLN BETA

Flight Plan: your day, tasks, OPORDs and a post map with hours, in one place.

Windows desktop app

⬇ Download FPLN for Windows Open FPLN in the browser
  1. Open the download, click Extract all, then double-click Open FPLN.cmd.
  2. When FPLN opens, click Install on the banner at the bottom (or the ⋯ menu › Apps › Install this site as an app).
  3. Right-click the FPLN icon on the taskbar › Pin to taskbar. Done. You can delete the download.

Updates install themselves the next time FPLN opens. You never download it again.

Phone

Open braveheart30.github.io/FPLN- in Safari or Chrome, then Share › Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ › Install app (Android).

Everything you put in FPLN stays on your own device. Keep CUI out of it (no FRAGORDs, rosters or other cadets' personal information). A cadet project, not an official USMA or U.S. Army product. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
