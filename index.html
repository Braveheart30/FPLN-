        Connect Canvas

        Bring your Canvas assignments, quizzes and due dates into Tasks. New ones are added, changed due dates are updated, and anything you've submitted is checked off.

        Sync from Canvas · works on any computer, no setup
        1. (sign in to Canvas if it asks)
        2. On the page that opens (a wall of text is normal), press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C.
        3. Come back here. FPLN adds your assignments by itself, or press
        Faster after setup: FPLN Sync button
        1. Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show your favorites bar, then drag this onto it: ⇄ FPLN Sync
          Can't drag it? , then Ctrl+Shift+O › … › Add favorite, name it FPLN Sync, paste into URL.
        2. On any Canvas page, click FPLN Sync.

        Canvas access token (API key)

        From Canvas: Account › Settings › + New access token. Saved only on this device. Canvas only accepts tokens from apps with their own server and turns websites like FPLN away, so use Sync from Canvas above. It uses your normal Canvas sign-in instead of the token.

        Pre-flight

        Load your plan

        Give FPLN everything: BUFF card, syllabi, course calendars, training notes, the mess hall menu, screenshots of emails. Claude reads it all and fills in your classes, deadlines, formations, uniform and menu. Nothing is added until you confirm.

        Keep CUI out: no FRAGORDs, rosters or other cadets' personal information.
